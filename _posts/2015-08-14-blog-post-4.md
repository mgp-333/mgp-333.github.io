Diffusion Models are based on the process of taking an image that has had noise added to it and learning the process of denoising it. Forward diffusion process takes a baseline image and adds noise to it.
Undoing this process is called reverse diffusion.
The forward diffusion process takes the form of a markov chain where the distribution at a particular timestep only depends on the sample output from the previous step.
So we can write out the distribution of corrupted samples conditioned on the inital data point as the product of sucessive single step conditionals. For continuous data, each transition is parameterized as a diagonal gaussian. 

The reverse diffusion process is marked by learning a model to approximate these conditional probabilities in order to run the reverse diffusion process.

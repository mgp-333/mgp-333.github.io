INTRO:
======
Tensor completion is the concept of completing or filling out missing/unobserved entries of partially observed tensors. Tensor completion has many applications in datamining, signal processing and more. 
Provides 4 perspectives of advances in tensor completion: general tensor completion, auxiliary information (variety), scalable tensor completion algorithms(volume), dynamic tensor completion algorithm 
(velocity).  
General Completion:
------
We can summarize general fundamental tensor completion approaches by splitting them into three main categories: decomposition based approaches, trace norm approaches and other approaches. 
Decomposition Based Approaches:
-There are two widely used decomposition CP and Tucker decomposition. 
CP decomposition
------
Rasmus Bro shows two ways of handling missing data in the CP decomposition model: Special expectation maximization algorithm, and missing skipping approach.
The EM approach assumes gaussian residuals and focuses on alternatively estimate the model parameters while imputing the missing data. 
The MS approach skips the missing values and builds up models using only observed entries. 


Tucker decomposition 
------
This approach seeks to approximate a core tensor that is multipled by factor matrices. 

Differences between the tucker and the CP decomposition based approachess to general tensor completion are that Tucker is more effective, CP is more unique, and more computationally flexible.

Trace Norm Based Approaches:
------
Tensor trace norm is the combinations of the trace norms of the unfoldings and reformulates the tensor rank minization problem as a convex optimization problem.
